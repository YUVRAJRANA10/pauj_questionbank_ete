# Q60-cse-salary-increment

A company maintains employee records containing employee ID, employee name, 
department, and salary. The management wants to provide a 10% salary increment to all 
employees working in the CSE department. 
Update the salary of every CSE employee by 10% and display the employee name, 
department, and updated salary. 
Input Format 
The first line contains an integer N, representing the number of employees. 
The next N lines contain: 
employee_id employee_name department salary 
The final line contains the department for which the salary increment must be applied. 
Output Format 
Display: 
employee_name department updated_salary 
for all employees in ascending order of employee_id. 
Constraints 
• 
1 ≤ N ≤ 100  
• 
Salary is a positive integer.  
• 
Employee names and department names contain no spaces.  
• 
The increment is exactly 10%.  
Sample Input 
4 
101 Amit CSE 50000 
102 Neha ECE 45000 
103 Rahul CSE 60000 

--- PAGE 50 ---
104 Simran IT 55000 
CSE 
Sample Output 
Amit CSE 55000.00 
Neha ECE 45000.00 
Rahul CSE 66000.00 
Simran IT 55000.00
